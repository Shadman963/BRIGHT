Q. What does the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) cover?

CSC Type A and Type B scholarships cover 100% of university tuition fees, free on-campus accommodation (single or double occupancy), comprehensive medical insurance, and a monthly living allowance of 2,500 RMB for Bachelor, 3,000 RMB for Master, and 3,500 RMB for PhD students.

Q. How can I track my application status with Bright Edu?

Once you submit your application through our online portal, you receive a Unique Application ID (e.g. BEC-2026-XXXXX). Log in anytime to view your live 7-step tracker, see verified documents, and receive notifications if any document needs a re-upload.

Q. Are MBBS programs in China recognized internationally?

Yes! We only partner with Chinese medical universities listed by the World Health Organization (WHO), World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS), BMDC, PMDC, and MCI/NMC, making graduates eligible to sit for licensing exams (USMLE, PLAB, AMC) worldwide.
